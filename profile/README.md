# Brown Sports Data 🏀📊

We're a student club at Brown University putting sports data into action
to boost athletic performance, learning, and community.

## What we work on
- **Analysis with Brown Athletics:** real data projects with varsity teams and Sports Performance
- **Prediction Markets:** research, strategy, and Brown's first intramural market
- **Sports Journalism:** articles on our research and the club website
